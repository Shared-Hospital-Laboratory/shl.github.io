## Working with our hospitals

SHL engages its member hospitals in clinical advisory, operations and planning.

We also work with Infection Prevention and Control (IPAC), Infectious Diseases (ID) and Antimicrobial Stewardship Programs (ASP) on antibiograms, committee representation and test utilization, all of which help us provide high-quality microbiology services.
