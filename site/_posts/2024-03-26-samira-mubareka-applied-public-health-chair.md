Dr. Samira Mubareka, an infectious disease physician, medical microbiologist and scientist at Sunnybrook and SHL, has received an Applied Public Health Chair, co-funded by the Canadian Institutes of Health Research (CIHR) and the Public Health Agency of Canada (PHAC).

Her chair, *Prevention of Zoonotic Pathogen Spillover at the Human–Animal Interface*, was one of the 2024 Applied Public Health Chairs.
